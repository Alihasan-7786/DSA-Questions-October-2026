
# Q4 - Product Popularity Ordering

**Difficulty:** Medium

## Problem Statement
Given n product IDs from sales records, arrange them according to these rules:

1. Products with smaller total frequency come first.
2. If two products have the same frequency, the larger product ID comes first.
3. All copies of the same product ID must appear consecutively.

Built-in sorting, Counter and sorted() with a key are not allowed. Write your own sorting routine with a custom comparison.

## Input Format
- First line contains an integer n.
- Second line contains n space-separated product IDs.

## Output Format
Print all product IDs in the required order on one line, separated by spaces.

## Constraints
- 1 <= n <= 10^5
- 1 <= ID <= 10^9

## Sample Input
9
4 4 6 2 2 2 6 9 6

## Sample Output
9 4 4 6 6 6 2 2 2
